No penalties

Compliance as a clause

Paybooks promises no penalties ever on the filings it handles.

No penalties ever. That is Paybooks’ published promise for the filings and payments it handles for your employees. The exact terms go in your service agreement.

  • Every payroll law, covered

    Provident fund, state insurance, professional tax, income tax withholding, labor welfare fund, gratuity, and the Labour Codes. In every state your people work in.

  • Filed on time, by chartered accountants

    A named chartered accountant on your account owns every deadline. Deductions follow the latest rules.

  • We pay the government directly

    You never receive a tax bill to pay. No gap for a penalty to grow in.

  • Monthly proof

    What was filed, when, with receipt numbers. Your auditor can use it.

  • Registrations, inspections, advice

    We register you with the provident fund, insurance, tax, and labor departments, represent you in inspections, and advise on pay structure.

  • Tax risk and IP

    We flag roles that create tax risk before you hire. IP is assigned to you.

    Letting one person go, or moving your team to your own entity. We handle both.

    • Day 0

      You decide. We confirm notice, usually 30 to 90 days.

    • Day 1 to 3

      We issue the termination letter under Indian law. Notice, or pay in place of notice.

    • Last day

      Final pay: salary, unused leave, gratuity if due.

    • After

    Moving to your own Indian entity

    • Decide

      Usually at 20 to 50 people. We set up and run the entity, or hand it to your team.

    • Weeks 1 to 12

      Your entity is set up. New hires keep joining through us.

    • Switch day

      Contracts move over. Provident fund and gratuity continue. Same payslip app.

    • After

      Same payroll team, now for your entity. Give us 30 days' notice to end the EOR.

    What to know before you hire in India.

    Is it legal for a foreign company to hire in India this way?

    Yes. Paybooks is an Indian company that employs your staff under Indian law and provides their services to you.

    Will senior candidates accept a Paybooks contract?

    Yes. Your company, manager, and role are on the letter. Benefits match a good Indian employer. We brief every candidate first.

    What does it really cost?

    Salary, plus about 8 to 9% in government costs, plus $199. The calculator above shows every line.

    Does this create tax risk for my company?

    It lowers it. Risk rises only if your India team signs contracts on your behalf. We flag those roles before you hire.

    Can EOR employees get our stock options?

    Yes, through a grant letter from your company.

    How does termination work in India?

    Notice is usually 30 to 90 days, or pay in place of notice. Gratuity is due after one year. We handle the process and the final pay.

    What happens when we set up our own Indian entity?

    We set it up and run it if you want. Contracts move over. Provident fund and gratuity continue.

    Who owns the IP?

    You do. It is assigned to you in the contracts.

    Which states do you cover?

    All of India.
