FINDINGS

Three findings decide the strategy.

Buyers search by destination country.

“Employer of record [country]” searches add up to 11,180 a month across 26 countries, almost as much as the core “employer of record” searches (11,520). 24 of Paybooks' 42 countries have measured demand, and Phase 6A carries 13,550 a month.

Leaders pair country pages with languages.

Of the eight EOR competitors Paybooks named, Deel publishes in 12 languages, Remote in 10 and G-P in 17, on top of 113 to 186 country pages each. Multiplier, Skuad, Velocity Global and Asanify are English-only.

Paybooks' plan has the right shape.

The alignment deck's country layer, 42 countries with an EOR page and a hiring guide each, is the model the leaders win with. Search adds the depth under each country, the role pages, the comparison set, and the buyer's language, where TransPerfect's own localization business is an advantage no EOR competitor has.

04 · PAYBOOKS TODAY

One country, fourteen pages, one keyword.

Paybooks' Employer of Record content covers India only: 14 pages and about 34,561 words, ranking for one keyword. The faults below are fixed in the India country cluster, and the new site starts clean everywhere else.

1country with an EOR page today
14EOR pages, all about India
1keyword ranking, “eor solutions” at #8 in India
8 issues on the current pages, most damaging first
#IssueEvidenceFix in the plan
1High
The same article is published twice.
"Employer of Record in India: Fast & Compliant Expansion for Startups" and "Why Global Tech Startups Are Using Employer of Record India in 2026" are 90% identical, word for word. The second is the first republished with the year changed, and the first still says 2025 on the page. Both are indexed, and a third startup article from 2024 targets the same search.Merge all three into the EOR India page and redirect.
2High
Two service pages compete for the same search.
/eor/ and /eor-2/ are both indexed, both in the sitemap, and each names itself as the main version. /eor-2/ is a thin 479-word page titled "EOR Services at Best Price", with two main headings, one a leftover template line: "Find the Information You're Looking For".Redirect /eor-2/ to the new EOR India page and drop it from the sitemap.
3High
The EOR service page's headline is about payroll.
The main heading on /eor/ reads "Paybooks is the Payroll Solution Choice of 3,000+ Leading Companies". The page has no meta description and is marked up as a blog article, not a service. Its only ranking search is "paybooks login", at #23.The new page leads with Employer of Record India, carries a description and service markup.
4High
None of the 12 EOR articles has a main heading.
The article template shows each title but does not mark it as the page's main heading (H1). Google loses the clearest signal of what each page is about.Fix the article template once; the fix carries to every article that uses it.
5Medium
The page that sells hides the price.
/eor/ shows no price and no tables at 1,697 words. Skuad ranks with $199 and 19 tables on the page.Show $199 and every statutory cost line, with a calculator.
6Medium
The articles are buried.
Each EOR article has just 2 internal links pointing to it. /eor/ receives 265 but links to almost none of them, including the 5,210-word hiring guide.Hub and spoke: every guide links to the EOR page and the EOR page links back.
7Medium
Two definition articles compete.
"What is Employer of Record (EOR) Services?" from 2024 and the 2026 "EOR Guide: Costs, Risks and Use Cases" answer the same question.Merge into one short glossary answer and redirect.
8Medium
The dates signal stale content.
The only page that ranks carries 2025 in its title. Two 2024 articles have never been updated since they were published.Refresh to 2026 with a visible reviewed date.

07 · INTERNATIONAL SEO

Every EOR search has two countries in it.

The buyer sits in one country and hires in another. A German company hiring in Poland searches “Employer of Record Polen”, in German. English pages win the English-speaking buyer markets; the buyer markets on the 42-country list that search in their own language need the same pages in that language.

English first, then the buyer's language

Launch in English on paybooks.transperfect.com. Then add the product home, the 13 modules and the 42 country EOR pages and hiring guides in five languages: German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese. That is 98 pages per language, 490 in all, each a real translation reviewed in-market.

The advantage no competitor has

TransPerfect's business is translation and localization: GlobalLink and translation in 200+ languages for 90% of the Fortune 500. Deel, Remote and G-P pay for their 12, 10 and 17 languages. Paybooks can localize faster, better and in-house.

Technical rules for every language

Folders, not domainsOne site, one authority: paybooks.transperfect.com/de/, /fr/, /es/, /pt/, /ja/. No separate country domains to build from zero.
hreflang on every page pairEach page lists every language version of itself, plus x-default pointing to English. Only for pages that exist in that language.
Translated slugs and metadataURLs, titles, descriptions and headings in the language, not English slugs with translated text.
Local currency and dates in toolsCalculators and cost tables show euros, yen or reais and local date formats, with the exchange date shown.
No redirects by locationVisitors and search engines choose the language; a language switcher on every page, no automatic redirects by IP.
Measured per languageA Search Console view per language folder, and rankings tracked in each market's Google, plus Naver for Korea.
Not built: English copies per regionen-gb, en-au or en-sg versions duplicate the same page. One English page serves every English-speaking buyer until content differs, such as local pricing.

09 · RULES

Hundreds of pages, none of them thin.

Templates make the scale possible. These rules keep every page worth ranking, in every language.

Real data on every pageEach country page carries its own employer costs, contract rules, benefits, notice periods and payroll dates, taken from the country's official sources and dated. A page without them stays unpublished.
Only countries Paybooks servesA country goes live only when Paybooks can employ there. Every page states what Paybooks does in that country.
Launch in batchesIndia first, then Phase 6A countries in order of measured searches. The next batch ships once the first is indexed and ranking.
Links in three directionsDown from the product home to each country, across between a country's pages, and up from every page to the product home and the quote form.
Translated by people, checked in-marketNo machine translation published unreviewed. A language goes live page by page, with hreflang added only when the page exists in that language.
Built for AI answersA short answer at the top of each page, question headings, tables and FAQ markup, so answer engines can quote Paybooks in every language.
Refreshed every yearRates, thresholds and holidays updated each year, with the review date shown on the page.
