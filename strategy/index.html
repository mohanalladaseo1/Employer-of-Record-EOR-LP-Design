The verdict

Three findings decide the strategy.

Authority is not what decides this category.

On "employer of record india", page one runs from DR 81 (Deel) down to DR 29 (Infotree). For "PEO India", sites at DR 12 to 37 hold #3 to #10. Paybooks at DR 41 is already strong enough to rank.

Depth on the right page decides it.

Skuad ranks #1 for the cost query with one 8,658-word page, 35 headings and 19 tables. Wisemonk published 666 India pages, and its EOR page reaches only #10 in India. One deep page per real intent beats hundreds of thin ones.

No competitor covers all five India intents well.

The commercial page, hiring guide, PEO question, provider comparison and role-based hiring are each won by a different competitor. Paybooks can be the one site that answers all five, with the lowest published price and 12 years of India payroll behind it.

Competitor playbooks

What each competitor built for India, from its sitemap, and what the rankings say about whether it works.

WisemonkDR 45Volume without traction

What it tells us. Little of it earns traffic. Its top US India page is a public-holidays blog, and its EOR page reaches only #10 in India for the head term. Scale without depth or authority does not win.

SkuadDR 60One very deep page

What they built. 21 India URLs, but one 8,658-word country page with 35 headings, 19 tables and a $199 price carries the cluster. Separate PEO, company-registration and role pages.

What it tells us. Ranks #1 for the cost query and #4 for the head term with only 21 pages. Depth on the money page is what works.

Papaya GlobalDR 72Guides that answer the question

What they built. 17 India URLs, almost all blog guides: EOR in India, how to hire, benefits, paying employees.

What it tells us. Top traffic winner: its EOR India guide earns about 240 visits a month, its hiring guide 70. Guides outrank service pages on these queries.

DeelDR 81Authority plus a few guides

What they built. 17 India URLs: two EOR India guides, a PEO vs EOR guide, payroll, costs, and a product landing page at $599.

What it tells us. Ranks #1 for PEO India and page one for the head term, but no India cost page, no city or state content, and a price three times Paybooks'.

RipplingDR 84Role-based hiring guides

What they built. 20 India URLs: EOR, PEO, benefits, termination, contractors, and "hire software developers in India".

What it tells us. Its biggest India page is the developer-hiring guide. It proves EOR vendors win role-based hiring searches.

RemoteDR 80Country explorer

What they built. 39 India URLs, mostly a templated country explorer: EOR, payroll, taxes, leave, termination, equity, and 8 benefits pages.

What it tells us. Broad but templated; does not place on page one for the India head terms measured.

MultiplierDR 68Templated country microsite

What they built. 52 India URLs from a template used for 100+ countries: EOR, payroll, laws, visas, subsidiary, compliance calendar.

What it tells us. Page one for the head term (#6) on template alone. Blocks crawlers, so pages were counted from its sitemap only.

AsanifyDR 48India specialist, blog-heavy

What they built. 139 India URLs: EOR cost, permanent establishment, onboarding, contractor payments by origin country, and 60+ role-hiring blogs.

What it tells us. Ranks #5 for the cost query with a dedicated cost page. Shows the India-specialist angle works on long-tail terms.

Why Paybooks can win this

The price is a ranking asset.

$199 a month against Deel's $599 and Remote's $699. Skuad ranks #1 for cost with $199 on the page. Paybooks can publish the same price with every statutory line in dollars, which no ranking page does.

Authority is already enough.

DR 41 sits above Infotree (29), Gloroots (38) and the PEO vendors (12 to 37) that already hold page one. Links from transperfect.com (DR 74) close the gap to Skuad (60) and Multiplier (68).

Proof competitors cannot copy.

3,000+ customers, 1.5 million payslips a year, 12 years of India payroll, and a written no-penalty guarantee. None of the eight competitors analyzed publishes a guarantee.

AI answers are the next battleground.

Google's AI Overview for "employer of record india" cites ten sources, including a Reddit thread and a LinkedIn post. Answer-first pages with FAQ schema and clear tables are what those answers quote.
